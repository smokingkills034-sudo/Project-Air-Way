PROJECT AIRWAY

ADVOCACY’S MOTTO

MISSION

Our team wants to raise awareness about inadequate classroom ventilation, encourage student participation, and promote cooperation among school administrators, Local Government Units (LGUs), and the community to provide practical and sustainable solutions to classroom heat.

VISION

Our team wants to create a safe, comfortable, and well-ventilated learning environment where every student can learn effectively without being hindered by excessive classroom heat.

VALUES

  • Responsibility
    Taking responsibility for identifying and addressing problems that affect the learning environment.
  • Cooperation
    Working together with students, school administrators, LGUs, and the community.
  • Empathy
    Understanding the discomfort and difficulties experienced by students in hot classrooms.
  • Initiative
    Taking action and proposing practical solutions instead of simply waiting for change.
  • Inclusivity
    Ensuring that every classroom and student receives attention, especially those experiencing inadequate ventilation.
  • Sustainability
    Promoting long-term improvements that maintain a comfortable learning environment.

ABOUT US

Hey there! We are a group of five Grade 12 students from Bangkal Senior High School, advocating for a more comfortable and well-ventilated learning environment.

This project originally started as a performance task for our Empowerment Technologies (EmpTech) subject. However, beyond fulfilling an academic requirement, we see it as an opportunity to address an issue that we personally experience as students.

Through this initiative, we aim to raise awareness, encourage collaboration, and promote practical solutions to the excessive heat and inadequate ventilation inside our classrooms.

GOAL

Our goal is similar to our vision, mission and values, as they serve as our compass in achieving our goal. This advocacy’s goal is to address the lack of proper classroom ventilation and reduce the discomfort caused by excessive heat.

We aim to encourage collective action among students, school administrators, and Local Government Units (LGUs) to help create a more comfortable and conducive learning environment.

WHY DID WE DO THIS?

Aside from doing this advocacy because it’s a performance task for our subject, every school day we enter our classrooms with the expectation of learning, yet excessive heat and insufficient ventilation can make it difficult to stay comfortable and concentrate, especially as the school day progresses. This can affect our ability to participate in discussions, understand lessons, and maintain focus until the final session of the day.

We believe that a comfortable learning environment should not be overlooked because it plays an important role in students’ learning experiences. Rather than simply accepting this as an ordinary classroom problem, we decided to use this opportunity to speak up, raise awareness, and propose possible solutions. Providing proper classroom ventilation is not merely about comfort; it is also about supporting students’ ability to learn.

Ultimately, this initiative represents our effort to turn a shared student concern into a collective call for improvement.
